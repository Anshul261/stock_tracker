1. Portfolio value leads its page; stock price and purchase baseline lead the stock view; Exposure leads with the direct allocation percentage; Scenarios leads with the loss and resulting value. Discover leads with its search and company list.
2. The canvas uses equal gutters and fills available width. The stable list is adjustable and collapsible. No page is dimmed behind stock or research content. Tables scroll within their frame when necessary on tablets; phone lists retain fields as sublines. The portfolio's first holding is now visible at 1440×900.
3. Selection marks are 1px blue lines. Chart data has one blue hue. Daily moves use semantic positive/negative colors in the Today columns. Negative scenario impact is a single semantic signal; the remaining financial figures stay neutral. No gradient surfaces, glass or tinted icon tiles.
4. Repeated stock prices, share counts, opening costs, weights, account balance, direct sector amounts and gains come from data.js. Totals use integer cents. USD 252,308.00 in positions plus USD 14,820.50 cash equals USD 267,128.50. USD 38,982.60 is unrealized price gain. Every delta states previous close, opening cost, invested capital or the chosen shock as its baseline. No sales, dividends, tax, fees or FX are implied.
5. Zero shock prints USD 0.00 in neutral text. Empty filters keep the list frame and offer a clear reset. Unsupported research questions explicitly explain the available calculations rather than pretending to answer them.
6. Price charts are generated by the vendored helper with a scaled y-axis, dated x-axis, straight segments, a labeled purchase-cost reference, and hover values. Exposure bars use the exact percentages printed at their ends. No legend is fabricated for a single series.
7. The phone rail becomes labeled bottom navigation. Stocks and research use the whole canvas. Discover's search is visible before its first row; tabs scroll within their own strip. Keyboard and pointer resizing, visible focus and tab arrow navigation were tested.
